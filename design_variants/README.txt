PDF design samples (programme + report for each), built from real archive films.

01-current              the PDFs as they are today
02-current-wider-gaps   today's layout with more space between films
03-editorial            serif text, red rule under the logo, hairlines between films
04-cards                each film in a card with a red edge, still on the left
05-band                 black header band with the white logo, big film numbers
06-minimal              black and white only, the most whitespace
07-cards-plain          like 04-cards, without the red strip on the left

Rebuild with: python scripts/build_design_variants.py
